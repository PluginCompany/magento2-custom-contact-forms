# Form Submissions

For each contact form you can choose to save its form submissions in the Admin Panel by enabling the 'Save form submissions' option in the [Form Settings](./09-form-settings.md) tab.

Every time a contact form is submitted, the form submission will be automatically saved in the back-end, where you can easily view its details and export submitted form data to CSV or Excel.

The forms submissions of a specific contact form can be managed in the Form Submissions tab of the form view.

![manage form submissions](images/M2-ContactForms10-1.png)

Alternatively, you can manage the form submissions of all your contact forms by navigating to **Content > Contact Forms > Form Submissions**.

![manage contact form submissions](images/M2-ContactForms10-2.png)

Select a form submission from the grid to view the submitted form data and copies of its customer and admin notification (if enabled).

![view form submission details](images/M2-ContactForms10-3.png)
